Getting started

Built-in guide

What is Streak OS?

Streak OS is a private, local-first habit and streak tracker. Everything lives inside this browser, protected by your password — nothing is sent to any server. It is fully bilingual (English/Persian), supports both Gregorian and Shamsi (Jalali) calendars, and turns daily logs into streaks, shields, trophies, and rich charts.

Use the 🌐 button in the toolbar to switch the language at any time. In Persian the whole interface, dates, and digits become RTL and Jalali.

First run & your vault

The first launch asks you to create a password. That password encrypts the vault — all activities, logs, and settings — inside the browser local database, so the stored data is unreadable without it. Press 🔒 to lock instantly, and after reloading the page unlock with the same password. You can change the password later in Settings → Security.

Toolbar & navigation

The toolbar holds the date controls: the ← and → buttons step one day, Today jumps to the current day, and the date field opens a popup calendar with its own year and month arrows plus Today and Clear buttons. ＋ Log selected day opens the logging form for the chosen date, ↶ and ↷ undo or redo changes, and 🔒 locks the app. The ☰ button in the corner collapses or expands the sidebar.

Your daily routine — a quick tutorial

A simple loop covers everything: pick the date (usually today), type each activity amount into its quick box and press Enter, then watch the status dot and progress bar update. When a day goes badly, open it later and log or edit the missing amounts — streaks, savers, and every chart recalculate instantly. End the week in Analytics to see totals, consistency, and the heatmap.

Quick log accepts the activity amount directly. The full Log dialog also supports date and note entry.

Logging & the workspace

Overview grid anatomy

Each row of the Daily workspace shows the activity emoji and name, its category, a colored status dot for the selected day, the current and best streak, a progress bar toward the daily target, today value, and a quick log box. The same status colors are used everywhere in the app, from the grid to History and Analytics, so one glance is always enough.

Quick log & the full log dialog

Type an amount and press Enter or the ✓ button to log instantly. The ＋ Log selected day button opens the full dialog where you can also change the date and attach a note. Every entry can be edited or deleted afterwards from the activity detail window, and each change is recorded in the change history.

Undo, redo & change history

Almost every change can be undone with ↶ or Ctrl/Cmd + Z and redone with ↷ or Ctrl/Cmd + Y. Settings → Change history lists every modification with its label and time and keeps the full history forever — the Show more button reveals older items beyond the recent ten. The same history can be exported as JSON or CSV from the Backup section.

Activities & rules

Creating an activity

＋ Add Activity opens the form. Choose a name, an optional category, the measurement type (time or count) with its unit, and a daily target. You can also set monthly savers, priority (lower number = higher), a minimum meaningful amount below which the day counts as a skip, initial current and best streaks for migration, and notes.

Time targets are normalized to minutes for comparison only. Displayed values are unchanged.

Editing without breaking history

When editing an existing activity, the "Changes effective from" date decides which days use the new settings: the chosen date and everything after it uses the new values, while earlier days keep their old configuration. This makes it safe to raise a target, change a unit, or rename an activity without corrupting past streaks and totals.

Pause, delete & bulk actions

Pausing an activity hides it from the daily grid while keeping its whole history; enable it again at any time. Deleting removes it from the grid but keeps the data, while permanent delete erases it for good. The checkboxes on each row let you apply bulk actions to many activities at once.

Sort, columns & search

In Activities → Sort & display you choose which columns are visible. Click a column header to sort by it, hold Shift while clicking to add more criteria, and drag the column borders to resize. The search box and the category filter narrow the grid, and the Times done column shows the all-time number of logs for each activity. Your layout is saved automatically.

Presets

Save the current program defaults, table setup, and sorting as a named preset from Settings → Settings & presets. Applying a preset restores that whole configuration in one click — handy when you experiment with new layouts or share the app with other people.

Streaks & savers

How streaks work

Reaching the daily target advances the current streak; logging less than the minimum meaningful amount makes the day behave like a skip. The best streak is remembered per activity, and the activity detail shows every past segment of the streak, ranked from the longest. A broken streak restarts from zero, but its records stay visible in the detail window.

Monthly savers 🛡️

Every activity owns a number of monthly savers. Missing a day consumes one saver and keeps the streak alive — such days are marked with a 🛡️ shield. Savers refill at the start of each month, and when the balance is empty the next miss breaks the streak with 💥. You can choose Gregorian or Shamsi months for the refill in Settings → Language & layout.

History & analytics

Browsing the calendar

The History tab renders the selected month as a calendar grid. Move between months with the arrows, click any day to open its full detail with every entry, amount, and note, and use the date field or the popup calendar to jump to any date. Days that hit every target celebrate with a 🏆 trophy.

Editing past days

Open any past day and log, edit, or delete entries exactly like today — scores, streaks, savers, and analytics update immediately. The selected-day label tells you whether you are looking at Today, a Past date, or a Future one, so backdated edits are never a surprise.

Analytics filters & KPI cards

The Analytics toolbar selects the range — the last 7, 30, or 90 days — plus the measurement type and unit. The five KPI cards summarize the selection: Total with a mini sparkline, Daily average, Best day with its date, a Consistency ring showing the share of active days, and the change versus the previous period of the same length.

Daily timeline & day breakdown

The timeline draws one bar per day with a dashed average line across the chart. Click any bar to drill into a stacked breakdown of that day and a ranked list of the activities behind it — the fastest way to answer "what exactly happened on this day?"

Unit charts

For every measurement type and unit, Analytics builds four graphic charts: 🏆 activity totals as ranked bars with each activity share, 🥧 a donut of the share of total, 📆 the weekday pattern as the average of each weekday, and 📈 the cumulative total as an area chart. Groups without data in the selected range stay hidden.

Chart colors follow the current theme, so changing the theme or accent recolors the whole dashboard.

Activity heatmap & year navigation

Pick an activity and the heatmap draws one cell per day for the whole year; a stronger color means a higher share of the target, and hovering a cell shows its date, amount, and percentage. Use the ‹ and › arrows beside the year to move between years in both directions — there is no one-year limit — and the Current year button jumps straight back. The pills above show the yearly total, times done, logged days, and the all-time total.

Settings & data

Language, direction & calendar

Settings → Language & layout switches every label between English and Persian. The calendar follows the language — Shamsi (Jalali) in Persian and Gregorian in English — and page direction can be automatic, forced RTL, or forced LTR. The saver reset calendar, Gregorian or Shamsi months, is also chosen here.

🎨 Themes & appearance

Seventeen theme presets restyle the whole app in one click, and custom colors fine-tune the accent, panels, and text. Sliders control the interface scale, corner radius, density, glow, and sidebar width. Charts, the heatmap, and every highlight follow these colors; press 💾 to save your appearance or reset to the defaults.

Backup & JSON database

The vault can live in the browser or in a portable JSON database file with optional encryption, so you can carry it between devices. Settings → Backup / import exports and restores full backups, and the activity history can additionally be exported as JSON or CSV. The Diagnostics log records technical messages for troubleshooting and can be copied with one click.

Security

Settings → Security lets you change the password used to unlock the vault. Locking with 🔒 instantly hides all data until the password is re-entered. Choosing an encrypted JSON database keeps everything unreadable even outside this app.

Cleanup (use with care)

Clear information resets activities and their history to the default activity set while keeping your password. Clear settings resets program defaults, table layout, sorting, presets, and tab positions. Both actions are irreversible — export a fresh backup first.

Make a fresh backup before any cleanup operation.

Keyboard & interaction tips

Enter in a Quick log field adds the amount. Ctrl/Cmd + Z undoes a change; Ctrl/Cmd + Y or Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + Z redoes it. Shift + click a main activity-grid header adds that column as the next sort criterion. In Overview, Savers, Target, Deficit, and Priority are independent columns and can be shown or hidden from Activities → Sort & display. In History, click a day for its full daily detail.

⚖️ Conflict policy & automation

Both devices changed the same part? The newer timestamp wins (Last-Write-Wins) and the losing version is always backed up — nothing is ever silently lost.

Auto-sync after every change
Timing: periodic sync runs every N seconds (minimum 30; 0 = only manual / on app open). With “Auto-sync after every change” on, each local change waits the configured delay, then syncs once. The cloud button in the top bar always syncs immediately when clicked.
Backups are stored in the “backups” folder of your repository. Old backups beyond the limit are deleted automatically.
